Our Philosophy

Crafted by Fire, Perfected by Tradition.

Founded by Master Chef Tariq Mahmood in the heart of Karachi, Zaiqa-e-Karachi was born from a simple conviction: Pakistani cuisine deserves the artistry, plating, and ingredient rigor of global fine dining without losing its soul.

We stone-grind our whole garam masalas every morning, source saffron directly from Pampore, and slow-cook our clay handis over aged tamarind wood charcoal for an unmistakable smoky depth.
